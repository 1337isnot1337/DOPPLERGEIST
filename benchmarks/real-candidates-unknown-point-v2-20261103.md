# Revised unknown-exact-point audio leads

This [five-source scanner-only shortlist](real-candidates-unknown-point-v2-20261103.csv)
replaces the ambiguous Lake Dunes excerpt in the
[original shortlist](real-candidates-unknown-point-20261103.csv) with a
[Cessna takeoff recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DqZ2411R1ys).
The original shortlist and its media remain unchanged. All five revised leads
show visually plausible descending tonal patterns; that visual judgment does
not independently establish aircraft identity.

The exact recorder coordinates and absolute recording time are unverified for
all five. Some sources disclose a broad area, including Syracuse or Puerto
Princesa Airport, so these are unknown **exact points**, not recordings with no
geographic clues. One source is explicitly a compilation and cannot establish
a locked clock across its apparent events. The Cessna uploader mentions
stabilization glitches, and its two overlapping scanner groups may be 2:3
harmonics of a single pass. Neither source is a validated multi-flyover
geolocation benchmark. The other clips also lack independent receiver/time
truth. No matching or geolocation was run on these sources.
