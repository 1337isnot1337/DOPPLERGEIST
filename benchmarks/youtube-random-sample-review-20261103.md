These notes describe the stored scanner ridge plots for the 24 proposal-bearing excerpts. The plot uses only automatic ridge outputs; it does not establish aircraft identity or location.

- Several clips show coherent descending traces. One outdoor-walk excerpt (`yt-m_iXOMuTTQw`) has a long smooth descending trace, but its physical source is unverified.
- The highest-count excerpt (`yt-f0tpROu3hr0`, 28 proposals) has a dense cluster of similarly directed traces. The indoor excerpt with the most proposals (`yt-klbzE_2LSEU`, 14) is also clustered. These could include harmonics, multiple sources, or non-aircraft artifacts; no labels were assigned.
- The other two indoor proposal excerpts have short low-frequency sweeps. The two phone-movement proposal excerpts also have short sweeps. Such traces show that indoor/handheld recordings can trigger the scanner, not that the scanner found aircraft Doppler there.
- The remaining 174 scanned excerpts had no proposal in the first 180 seconds; the rest of each video was not scanned. Two clips failed download and were retained as failures.

No audio was reprocessed for this review. No flight matching, geolocation, ground-truth lookup, or synthetic input was used.
