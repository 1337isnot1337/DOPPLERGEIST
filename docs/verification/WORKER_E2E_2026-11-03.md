## Historical bound verification on six failed tiles

This work preceded the owner directive.
It replayed six archived tiles from the earlier synthetic four-event HTTP run at source `134f617`.
The production bound source was `7a96fdb33fb787a9376432b2086f3673a4f4dc37`.
The replay used the archived discovery, flight index, tracks and terrain.
It did not load location truth or change scores.

For an association with `k` matched groups, the declared score is:

\[
S=\sum_{i=1}^{k}\left(1-\frac{\ell_i}{L_i}\right),\qquad L_i>0,\quad \ell_i\geq0.
\]

A validated event loss greater than its limit rejects the entire association.
For each unknown event loss, its score contribution cannot exceed one.
Validated supported losses can therefore tighten the general bound `S ≤ k`.
The candidate bound covers every scheduled clock trial.
The implementation rounds contributions and sums upward to protect the floating-point bound.

The code requires the declared score kind and validates the event diagnostics before it uses a loss.
It also validates disjoint trial counts and their sum against the scheduled count.
Unsupported motion errors remain unbounded.
Missing support remains unknown despite a finite score bound.
The bound applies to the enumerated grid and scheduled clocks, without a continuous-space claim.

## NASA measured recordings: negative automatic-discovery baseline

Original files came from NASA's [Small UAS flyover acoustics release](https://data.nasa.gov/dataset/small-uas-flyover-acoustics-data).
The first lexical Cub and Edge recordings were selected before acoustic inspection:
`cub_flyover_107.mat` and `edge_flyover_038.mat`. Downloaded original lengths,
ZIP CRCs and SHA-256 checks passed. The publisher supplies measured GPS trajectories,
microphone positions and acoustic sample times synchronized to the trajectory's
UTC seconds-of-day. The calendar day is not securely bound, so this experiment
uses the common session time axis and invents no absolute recording date.

The experimental adapter retained every GPS row and original pressure sample,
used the preselected second microphone channel, and ran the unchanged automatic
detector without event windows or bands. Its planned search grid came from the
aircraft's measured footprint plus a 1 km margin, with microphone truth in a
separate evaluation file opened only after the search was frozen. This is a
limited measured-trajectory experiment, not national flight acquisition.

| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |

| Edge038 | 59.4094 s | 0 | No fit or location |
| Edge065 | 43.8950 s | 1 | Ambiguous conditional fit; source association unconfirmed |

Both baseline runs completed. No distance metric or successful acceptance can be
assigned to these misses. Subsequent diagnosis found that nearly all tracked
fragments failed the detector's 12-second duration rule; the three paths surviving
the other shape gates failed the jump rule. Those rules were not relaxed.
Cub107 contains more than one close approach; the adapter's original one-flyover
label was an assumption, not a measured encounter count, and is corrected to
unknown in the versioned extension.

The extension predeclared **both** publisher `Edge / high speed` rows,
Edge065 and Edge066, before downloading their audio. The selection retained 065
despite its background-aircraft note. Discovery and search settings stayed unchanged.
Both cases completed preparation, search and evaluation; search took 6.560 s.

Edge065's sole proposal covered 1.5–19.0 s near 94–102 Hz. Both the constant-source
and two-percent-drift searches ranked the same 50 m grid cell first, **474.004 m**
from the supplied microphone position. That conditional error is outside 100 m and
inside 500/1,000 m. It is **not validated drone localization**: 568 of 4,200 grid
locations lacked fit support, a competing minimum was 1,899 m from the microphone,
and the descriptive near-best region extended over 2.3 km from the leader.

Post-freeze source diagnosis found the measured drone's nominal received closest
approach near 30.57 s. The early proposal was visibly separate from the strong
harmonic flyover around that time and consistent with the publisher's warning
about a background propeller aircraft at the recording's start. Its actual
identity remains unknown. A small conditional location error cannot validate a
wrong or unconfirmed source association.

A single controlled development comparison changed only the spectral window
from 2.0 s to 0.5 s and the hop from 0.5 s to 0.125 s. Every other acceptance setting
stayed unchanged. **All four recordings then produced zero proposals.** This
supplied no positive evidence for changing production defaults, which remain unchanged.
