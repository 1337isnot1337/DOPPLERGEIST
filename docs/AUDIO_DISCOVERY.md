# Automatic audio-event discovery

`dopplergeist discover input.mp3 --output results/new-audio-scan` scans the complete recording for acoustic moving-tone proposals. Automatic analysis uses the same discovery path. No event windows, frequency bands, aircraft identities or location hints are required.

Intake decodes the original audio at 4 kHz and 24 kHz. Discovery combines ridge tracking and time-frequency sequence analysis, verifies measured tracks, and groups related tracks into proposals. The lower-rate ridge finder remains an internal component of this detector. The two decodes are checked against the same source hash.

`discovery/discovery.json` contains the input hash, detector settings, canonical proposal intervals and measured child tracks. Tracks belonging to a proposal share a `harmonic_group_id`; downstream association should count the group once while retaining its measured tracks. Times are relative to the first decoded sample. `--plot-summary` renders a track overview and `--plot-event-details` renders individual track plots. Use a new output directory for each run.

Automatic analyses also write per-ridge spectrogram crops, ridge CSVs, bounded raw audio excerpts, illustrative band-limited excerpts, one compounded curve drawing per harmonic group, and `discovery/event-media.json`. The group drawing overlays every measured ridge in the group on a shared time/frequency axis, using a log frequency axis for wide-band groups, covering the full observed duration and breaking each ridge at its measured gaps. The raw excerpt is the primary listening evidence; the band-limited excerpt is not used by detection or scoring. Media failures are recorded in the index and do not stop the analysis. `discover` can render per-event plots with `--plot-event-details`; audio excerpts are generated by the automatic upload path.

Per-event plots use the event's recorded detector `window_s` when present. Otherwise they use the low-band display settings. The plot is oversampled to at most 400 time columns with a 0.05 s minimum display hop, so a short crop is not reduced to a few wide blocks. This adds display detail, not detector information: the analysis window still determines the real time/frequency resolution. Individual spectrogram crops are limited to 80 seconds; longer events are labelled as centered excerpts. The saved crop bounds, truncation flag, display window, hop, gap threshold and column count are recorded in `event-media.json`.

Measured ridge lines are broken at gaps above `max(0.5 s, min(1.5 s, 2 × detector hop))`; the browser also flags these gaps and non-monotonic direction. A break means the points after it are a separate measured fragment, not a continuation of the same curve. The flag is a review aid, not a detector rejection: non-monotonic tones can come from maneuvering aircraft or changing source frequencies.

Discovery uses fixed settings. A track is acoustic evidence, not proof that the source is an aircraft or that the pitch change was caused by physical motion. Missed, fragmented, merged and spurious proposals remain possible. Runtime, memory and disk use depend on recording length and content. Historical results from earlier discovery versions do not establish this detector's general performance or location accuracy.
